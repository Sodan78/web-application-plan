# Intent: couples check-in app for attachment security
Author: Gustav Arrhenius. Status: draft.

## Problem
Couples repeat the same painful patterns without understanding why.
Attachment insecurity drives much of it, but partners rarely see
their own or each other's patterns, and hard topics go unspoken.

## Proposed outcome
A couple uses the app together for regular check-ins. Each partner
starts with a self-assessment of attachment tendencies, then reflects
privately in each check-in and chooses what to share with the other.
Over time the app notices patterns in the reflections and helps each
person reflect toward more secure ways of relating.

Success after three months of use: the couple feels safer bringing
up hard topics, and both partners can name their own and each
other's patterns when they show up.

## Affected users and systems
Couples in a relationship who are in couples therapy, using the app
together as part of that therapy. Both partners have their own
account. By default only the two partners have access; the couple
can give their therapist access. New product, no existing systems.

## Constraints
- Private first: each partner reflects alone; nothing is shown to
  the other partner unless they choose to share it.
- Gentle disclosure: no attachment label is shown right after the
  self-assessment. The picture builds gradually through check-ins,
  framed as tendencies in situations, not a fixed type.
- Reflections are sensitive personal data under GDPR.
- Out of scope: relationships with abuse or control; support for
  conflict in the moment.

## Open questions
- Therapist access: does it need consent from both partners, and
  what does the therapist see once given access?
- Does being part of therapy make the app a regulated medical
  device (EU MDR), and how do we stay on the right side of that?
- How does the app detect signs of abuse or control, and where does
  it point people instead?
- Which self-assessment instrument, and is it licensed for this use?
- What happens to shared and private data if the couple separates?
